What We Do
Two Medicare-covered services working together

Preventive Care

Annual Wellness Visit (AWV)

A preventive visit that helps a clinician review your health risks and create or update a personalized prevention plan. It is not the same as a traditional physical examination. Eligible patients may be able to complete the visit from home by telephone or secure video.

  • Health risk assessment
  • Medical history & medication review
  • Screening for depression, cognitive concerns, fall risks
  • Personalized prevention plan
  • Referrals for preventive services
🩹
Care Coordination

Community Health Integration (CHI)

A healthcare service for certain patients whose medical care is being affected by everyday barriers. Depending on your needs, it may include care coordination, health education, help navigating healthcare services, and assistance identifying community resources related to your medical care. A licensed clinician must decide whether the service is medically appropriate.

  • Person-centered planning & goal setting
  • Health system coordination & navigation
  • Patient education & self-advocacy support
  • Community resource identification
  • Ongoing follow-up & care coordination

Cost & Coverage
Understanding what Medicare covers
The Annual Wellness Visit and Community Health Integration have different cost structures under Medicare.

Annual Wellness Visit

Generally no cost

The covered Annual Wellness Visit generally has no patient cost under Original Medicare when you are eligible and the provider accepts assignment. Separate services performed during the visit may have a cost, so coverage should always be verified.

Community Health Integration

Part B cost sharing may apply

For Community Health Integration, the normal Medicare Part B deductible and coinsurance may apply. Your actual cost depends on your insurance coverage, including whether you have met your deductible, have supplemental insurance, or are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan.

Medcare does not guarantee specific coverage outcomes. Eligibility and coverage should always be verified. Cost sharing details depend on your individual Medicare plan and coverage.
