1. Overview and Effective Date

This Privacy Policy describes how Medcare ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal and health information when you visit our website at medcaretelehealth.org, use our online tools such as the eligibility checker or schedule form, or receive services through our telehealth platform.

Medcare is an independent telehealth healthcare provider. We are not Medicare, Medicaid, CMS, a government agency, an insurance company, or a government assistance program.

This policy is effective as of July 27, 2026. We may update this policy from time to time, and we will post the updated version on this page with a revised date.

2. HIPAA Notice

Medcare complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations, including the HIPAA Privacy Rule and Security Rule. We maintain the privacy and security of your Protected Health Information (PHI) in accordance with federal law.

Our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes how we may use and disclose your health information for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations, is available upon request. To request a copy, contact us using the information in Section 13 below.

Your health information is protected under federal law. We will not share your Protected Health Information without your written authorization, except as permitted or required by HIPAA.

3. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you use our website tools or services, we may collect:

  • Personal identifiers: First name, last name, date of birth
  • Contact information: Phone number, email address, mailing address (if provided)
  • Eligibility information: Information you submit through the eligibility checker, such as name and date of birth, used to verify Medicare coverage
  • Scheduling preferences: Preferred date and time for a visit
  • Consent records: Your agreement to be contacted and to have your information verified

Health Information

During a telehealth visit, our clinical team may collect health information relevant to your care, including medical history, current medications, symptoms, and screening results. This information becomes part of your medical record and is treated as Protected Health Information under HIPAA.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Approximate geographic location (derived from IP address)

This information is collected through Google Analytics and is used in aggregate, de-identified form to understand website usage patterns and improve our services.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Eligibility verification: To check whether you are eligible for a Medicare Annual Wellness Visit or other services
  • Scheduling and care delivery: To schedule appointments and provide telehealth services
  • Communication: To contact you about your eligibility results, appointment confirmations, and follow-up care
  • Care coordination: To coordinate your care with clinical staff and, when appropriate, connect you with community resources
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable federal and state laws, including Medicare billing requirements
  • Website improvement: To analyze website usage and improve user experience
  • Security and fraud prevention: To protect our website and services from unauthorized access or fraudulent activity

5. When We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal or health information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

With Your Authorization

We will not disclose your Protected Health Information for purposes other than treatment, payment, and healthcare operations without your written authorization. You may revoke your authorization at any time in writing.

For Treatment and Care Coordination

We may share your health information with your primary care provider or other healthcare professionals involved in your care, with your consent.

With Business Associates

We work with third-party service providers who help us operate our business, such as eligibility verification services, email delivery, and analytics. These providers are bound by Business Associate Agreements under HIPAA and are required to protect your information.

As Required by Law

We may disclose your information when required by federal or state law, court order, or law enforcement, or to report suspected fraud, abuse, or public health concerns.

With Medicare or CMS

We may share information with Medicare or the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) as required for billing, eligibility verification, or compliance purposes.

6. Eligibility Verification

When you use our online eligibility checker, you provide your name, date of birth, and optionally your phone number and email. We use this information to verify your Medicare coverage through a third-party eligibility verification service.

Your consent is required before we perform any eligibility check. If you do not consent, we will not verify your eligibility or submit your information to any third party.

The results of your eligibility check, including whether you are eligible or not eligible, are logged in our internal records for follow-up and quality assurance purposes. We do not store your Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI) or Social Security Number on our website servers.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate properly and to understand how visitors use our site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect de-identified, aggregate data about website traffic, page views, and user behavior. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used to improve our website and is not used to identify you personally.

You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Privacy and Terms page. You may opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Essential Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function properly, such as remembering your preferences. These cookies do not collect personal information.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our website.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services to you and to comply with legal requirements:

  • Medical records: Retained in accordance with applicable state and federal medical record retention requirements
  • Eligibility check data: Retained for a reasonable period for follow-up and quality assurance, then deleted or de-identified
  • Website analytics data: Retained in aggregate, de-identified form in accordance with Google's data retention policies
  • Contact information: Retained as long as you have an active relationship with Medcare, or as needed to respond to your inquiries

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time, subject to legal retention requirements for medical records.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of your personal and health information that we hold
  • Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restriction: You may request that we restrict the processing of your information in certain circumstances
  • Portability: You may request a copy of your information in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent: You may withdraw your consent to processing at any time
  • Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights if you believe your privacy rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 13 below.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your personal and health information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These safeguards include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Access controls limiting information access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security assessments and staff training
  • Business Associate Agreements with all third-party service providers who handle PHI
  • Compliance with the HIPAA Security Rule

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100 percent secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a data breach affecting your Protected Health Information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable federal and state laws.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete that information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If we make material changes that affect how we use your information, we will provide a more prominent notice on our website or notify you directly if we have your contact information.
