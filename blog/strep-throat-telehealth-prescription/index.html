Can You Get a Strep Throat Telehealth Prescription? What to Know

By Medcare Telehealth  •  2026-07-24  •  5 min read

Waking up with a raw, scratchy throat and trouble swallowing often means one thing: you might have strep throat. It’s painful, contagious, and tends to hit fast. But before you sit in a crowded waiting room, you may be wondering: can I get a strep throat telehealth prescription? The short answer is yes—under the right circumstances. At Medcare Telehealth, our board-certified nurse practitioners licensed in New York and Florida can evaluate your symptoms online and, if clinically appropriate, send a prescription to your local pharmacy. Here’s how it works and what you need to know.

What Is Strep Throat and How Is It Diagnosed?

Strep throat is a bacterial infection caused by Group A Streptococcus bacteria. It’s different from a viral sore throat, which usually gets better on its own. Common symptoms include:

  • Sudden, severe sore throat
  • Pain when swallowing
  • Red and swollen tonsils, sometimes with white patches
  • Fever (often over 101°F)
  • Swollen lymph nodes in the neck

Because strep throat requires antibiotics to prevent complications and reduce spread, accurate diagnosis matters. During a telehealth visit, your provider will ask about your symptoms, medical history, and recent exposures. While a home test kit isn’t required, some patients choose to use an at-home strep test beforehand—but the provider will make the final clinical decision based on your full picture.

How Telehealth for Strep Throat Works

At Medcare Telehealth, the process is straightforward and patient-centered. When you book an urgent care visit, you’ll meet one-on-one with a licensed NP via secure video. The provider will:

  • Review your symptoms and duration
  • Ask about fever, pain level, and any breathing or swallowing difficulties
  • Check for red flags like difficulty opening your mouth or drooling (rare but serious)
  • Discuss whether a strep throat telehealth prescription is appropriate

If the NP determines that strep is likely and antibiotics are indicated, they can send a prescription to your preferred pharmacy—often within minutes. You’ll also receive aftercare advice, including pain management tips and when to follow up if symptoms worsen. Remember, not everyone with a sore throat needs antibiotics; if the provider suspects a virus, they’ll recommend supportive care instead.

When a Telehealth Prescription May Not Be Enough

While telehealth is convenient, it’s not right for every situation. You may need an in-person visit if you:

  • Have severe pain or trouble swallowing liquids
  • Notice a high fever that doesn’t improve
  • Have a history of rheumatic fever or other complications
  • Are immunocompromised or pregnant
  • Experience difficulty breathing or drooling (emergency signs)

Your Medcare Telehealth provider will guide you. If they feel an in-person strep test or throat culture is necessary, they’ll advise you on next steps—and can often help coordinate care. Your safety always comes first.
