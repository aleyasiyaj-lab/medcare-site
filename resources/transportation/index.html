Transportation Help for Seniors on Medicare

Transportation is one of the most common barriers seniors face in accessing healthcare. When an older adult can no longer drive, or has no reliable way to get to medical appointments, their health can deteriorate quickly. Medicare covers certain transportation services, and Community Health Integration (CHI) can help eligible patients navigate local transportation resources. This guide covers all transportation options available to seniors.
