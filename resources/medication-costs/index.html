Medication Cost Help for Seniors on Medicare

Prescription medication costs are a leading cause of financial stress for seniors on Medicare. Many older adults ration pills, skip doses, or go without essential medications because they cannot afford them. Medicare offers several programs that can help reduce prescription costs, and Community Health Integration (CHI) services can connect eligible patients with additional resources. This guide covers every major medication assistance program.
