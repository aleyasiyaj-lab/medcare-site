Caregiver Support Resources for Senior Loved Ones

Caregivers are the unsung heroes of senior care. Millions of adult children and family members provide unpaid care for aging parents, often while managing their own jobs, families, and health. Caregiver burnout is real, and the healthcare system can be overwhelming to navigate. Medicare covers services like Community Health Integration (CHI) that can help both seniors and their caregivers. This guide covers everything caregivers need to know.
