Senior Isolation: Health Effects, Warning Signs, and Help

Social isolation is a silent health crisis among seniors. Research shows that prolonged loneliness can be as harmful to health as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Older adults who live alone, have limited mobility, or have lost their social networks are at particular risk. Medicare covers services like Community Health Integration (CHI) that can help address isolation as part of a patient's overall care. This guide covers the health effects, warning signs, and resources available.
