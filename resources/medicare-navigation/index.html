Medicare Navigation: Understanding Your Benefits

Medicare is complex, and many seniors do not use benefits they are entitled to simply because they do not know about them. Annual Wellness Visits (AWV), Community Health Integration (CHI), and chronic care management services are all Medicare-covered benefits that can improve health outcomes and quality of life. This guide helps seniors and caregivers understand what Medicare covers and how to access these services.
