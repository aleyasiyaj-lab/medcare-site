Who Is Generally Eligible for an Annual Wellness Visit

Medicare beneficiaries are generally eligible for an Annual Wellness Visit once they have been enrolled in Medicare Part B for at least 12 months. Coverage rules can change, and individual situations vary, so it helps to confirm your eligibility with our team or your plan before scheduling.

What to Have Ready Before You Check

  • Your legal first and last name as they appear on your Medicare card
  • Your date of birth
  • A phone number or email where we can reach you with results

What Happens After You Check

If you are eligible, our team can help connect you with a licensed clinician to complete your Annual Wellness Visit by phone or secure video. During the visit, the clinician may also identify whether Community Health Integration services could help address barriers affecting your medical care.
